**A personal health tracker that turns what you record into clear summaries you can bring to your clinician.**

MultiUnity keeps symptoms, mood, food and drinks, medications and other interventions, vitals, observations, and screening questionnaires in one timeline. From those entries it builds charts, nutrition estimates, and short summaries, so it is easier to notice patterns and to describe what has been happening at your next appointment.

## What you can do

- **Track what matters to you:** rate symptoms and mood from 0 to 100, log meals and drinks, record medications and supplements with reminders, and add vitals or notes.
- **See your history:** graphs over time, pie charts, and a calendar of past entries.
- **Get nutrition estimates:** percent of daily values for calories, fiber, added sugar, protein, fat, and more, from what you type or dictate.
- **Prepare for appointments:** summaries and suggested questions for your clinician, and ChatGuide for questions about your own entries.
- **Stay in control:** download your data or delete your account at any time.

## Privacy

Your entries are stored in your account, encrypted in transit and at rest, and only you can see them. MultiUnity does not sell your information or use it for advertising. To generate summaries and estimates, the entries needed are processed by Anthropic's Claude, which does not use them to train its models.

## Not medical advice

MultiUnity organizes what you record. It does not diagnose, recommend treatments, or watch for emergencies. If you may be having a medical emergency, call 911. If you are thinking about suicide or hurting yourself, call or text 988.

## Status
